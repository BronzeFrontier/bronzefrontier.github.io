The Story So Far

Every journey starts in Mitis Town, where the player and their childhood friend, Rival Jake, receive their first Pokémon from the region's newly-promoted researcher, Professor Cypress. Before the player can get far down Route 1, tragedy strikes — their parents are abducted by the criminal organization Team Eclipse, kicking off a region-wide journey that's equal parts badge quest and rescue mission.

Along the way, players collect eight Gym Badges, clash repeatedly with Team Eclipse, and butt heads with a second rival, Tess, all while exploring a region that stretches from port towns and beaches to frozen mountains and haunted gyms.

Roria

Roria is the region at the heart of Bronze Frontier. It's a varied landmass — forests and archaeology sites near the starting town, coastal beach towns, a multi-district capital city, mountain passes, and a chain of outlying islands reachable by boat once the player is further into their journey.

The player's adventure begins in Mitis Town, a quiet starting point with a Pokémon Lab but no Pokémon Center — instead, a local resident on the bridge to Route 1 heals Trainers' teams by hand. From there, the region opens up gym by gym, city by city, all the way to the final Gym on Crescent Island.

Rivals

The player's childhood friend from Mitis Town. He picks Eevee as his own starter and challenges the player to their very first battle before the journey properly begins.

Starter Pokémon

Mitis Town's Pokémon Lab does things a little differently — instead of picking between just three starters, new Trainers choose from a whole roster spanning the classic Grass/Fire/Water trios of multiple core-series regions, all offered by the newly-promoted Professor Cypress and his assistant, David.

Rival Jake breaks from tradition entirely, choosing an Eevee as his own starter — and it's no pushover in your first battle.

Bronze Frontier's later-gen additions mean the starter roster — and Jake's team — may grow further. Log the specifics on What's New Here.

Legendary Pokémon

Roria is home to the same stationary Legendary Pokémon found in the original Pokémon Brick Bronze, each guarding a chamber, cavern, or landmark somewhere across the region. Italicized encounter notes mean the Pokémon can only be seen in that spot rather than caught, and "→ Roaming Pokémon" means it becomes a rare roaming encounter after its first Set Encounter.

The cards tagged Bronze Frontier Addition are new to this project — Ho-Oh, Regieleki, Zygarde, Chi-Yu, and Kubfu. Their encounter methods are intentionally left blank for now.

Mythical Pokémon

Just like the original Pokémon Brick Bronze, Roria is also home to a handful of elusive Mythical Pokémon, each with its own quest or hidden encounter. Italicized encounter notes mean the Pokémon can only be seen in that spot rather than caught, and "→ Roaming Pokémon" means it becomes a rare roaming encounter after its first Set Encounter.

The card tagged Bronze Frontier Addition is new to this project — Magearna. Its encounter method is intentionally left blank for now.

Daily Bonus Tracker

Bronze Frontier rotates a bonus encounter rate through the week. Wednesdays boost odds for wild Pokémon carrying their Hidden Ability. Friday through Sunday boosts Shiny odds instead. Every boost is tracked on the Eastern Time (EST/EDT) calendar day — so, for example, the Wednesday boost kicks in the instant the clock hits 12:00 AM Eastern on Wednesday, no matter what time zone you're playing from.
